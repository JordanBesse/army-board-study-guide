Army NCO Prep — Privacy Policy

Effective 1 August 2026

Army NCO Prep is built to work entirely on your device, and your studying stays private.

Information we collect

None. Army NCO Prep does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. There is no account, no login, and no sign-up.

On-device data

Your quiz scores, notes, highlights, bookmarks, and settings are stored only on your device. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use analytics, advertising identifiers, cookies, or any third-party tracking. The app does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Network use

The app works fully offline. The only time it uses the internet is when you choose to download or open a public Army regulation (PDF) from the reference library. These are ordinary requests to retrieve public U.S. Government publications; we do not attach any identifying information to them.

Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or social-networking services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email jb.devteam@pm.me.

Army NCO Prep is an independent study aid. It is not an official U.S. Army or U.S. Government application and is not endorsed by them. All doctrine referenced is publicly releasable.
