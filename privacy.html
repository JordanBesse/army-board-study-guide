Privacy Policy

Army Board Study Guide · Effective 28 July 2026

This app collects no personal information. It runs entirely on your device, and nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests to us or to any third party, and it works fully offline.

What is stored, and where

Your study progress — quiz scores, missed questions, your chosen board, and your display settings (theme, colour-blind palette, text-to-speech) — is saved only in your own device's local browser storage. It stays on your device. It is never transmitted, uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. We cannot see it.

You can erase all of it at any time using Reset study data in the app's Settings. Deleting the app removes it as well.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, software development kits, or trackers. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed, because none is collected in the first place. If you use the in-app feedback link, it opens your own email app or web browser — your message goes directly to us through your own account, and only if you choose to send it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Because the app collects nothing, substantive changes are unlikely.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to jb.devteam@pm.me.

Army Board Study Guide is an independent study aid. It is not an official U.S. Army or U.S. Government application and is not endorsed by them. All doctrine referenced is publicly releasable.
