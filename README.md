# Army Board Study Guide

An offline study guide for the **Soldier of the Month**, **promotion (SPC to SGT and SGT to SSG)**,
and **Sergeant Audie Murphy Club** boards. 455 multiple-choice questions across 26 categories, each
with a source citation and a short explanation.

## Use it on your phone

**[Open the study guide](https://jordanbesse.github.io/army-board-study-guide/)**

On iPhone: open that link in Safari, tap the **Share** icon, and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
Launch it from the new icon. It works offline after the first visit.

On Android: open the link in Chrome and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

## Prefer a single file?

Download [army-board-study-guide.html](https://jordanbesse.github.io/army-board-study-guide/army-board-study-guide.html)
and open it in any desktop browser. It is fully self-contained and works with no internet connection.

## What it does

- Four board types, each drawing a different mix of questions
- Quick, medium, and long quizzes with immediate scoring and a per-category breakdown
- Practice Missed Questions, with spaced repetition: an item leaves the set after two correct answers in a row
- A readiness score per board
- Flashcard review, an optional timer, and a printable study sheet
- Light and dark themes; all progress is stored on your own device

## Accuracy

This is a study aid. Verify every standard against the current regulation before a board.
Regulations change, and some figures (particularly Army Fitness Test scoring) are worth confirming
against the current AR 350-1 before you rely on them.

No data is collected and nothing leaves your device. The page makes no network requests.
